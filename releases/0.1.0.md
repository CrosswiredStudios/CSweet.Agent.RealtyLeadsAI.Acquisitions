# 0.1.0

First release of the Acquisitions Manager (SDK 5.8.0, plugin ≥ 0.2.0, works with Lead Research Analyst ≥ 0.2.0).

- Owner chat intake: deterministic `research-brief.v1`, `pipeline.v1` and `outreach-draft.v1` commands, status
  questions, and a bounded model rewrite of plain-language research requests with grounding.
- Delegates research briefs to the Lead Research Analyst with tracking; ingests delivered lead briefs into one
  pipeline card per lead on the manager's workboard, idempotently.
- Pipeline stages New → Qualified → Outreach planned → Contacted → Negotiating → Closed/Dead; terminal stages archive.
- Outreach drafts saved as `realtyleads.outreach-draft.v1` documents for owner approval; nothing is sent.
- Management-cycle status report and an `acquisitions.pipeline.summary.v1` capability for other agents.
